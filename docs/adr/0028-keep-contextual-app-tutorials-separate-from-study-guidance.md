# Keep contextual app tutorials separate from Study guidance

Altitutor UCAT presents first-visit contextual app tutorials through a dedicated, sampler-style tutorial coach while the Study guidance orb remains responsible for Study plan and next-step guidance. The tutorial may highlight the real orb and ask the student to open it, but it does not make the optional orb own tutorial state, targeting, completion, or replay; this avoids collisions with the orb's guidance, notices, and celebrations while keeping tutorials available when Study suggestions are disabled.
