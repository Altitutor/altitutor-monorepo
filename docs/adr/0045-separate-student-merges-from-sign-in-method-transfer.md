# Separate Student merges from sign-in method transfer

An administrator-confirmed Student merge retains one authentication account and its existing sign-in methods while consolidating the person's Student records. Retiring another account's Student access must preserve unrelated roles; transferring Apple, Google, or email/password access between authentication accounts is a separate student-assisted flow, because merging business records does not prove control of both credentials.

The retained Student, retained login, and customer used for future billing are independent choices. Historical billing customer mappings and financial evidence survive the merge so that older invoices and payment events remain attributable; a merge does not move payment methods between Stripe customers or silently cancel subscriptions.

Historical phone conversations retain an explicit relationship to the combined Student, while only the selected current phone remains an active contact. This preserves message history without multiplying automation recipients.
