# Retain the Student on product account deletion

Product account deletion closes one Product app's relationship and removes that product's learning data. It does not delete the Student. Invoices and attendance reference the Student and are removed with that row, and those records are kept for accounting and audit. The login is removed only when no other relationship needs it. A later signup with the same email reopens that product on the same Student and does not restore the deleted learning data.

Grouped attempt history stays personal information after the name, email, and phone are erased, so it is deleted rather than kept for percentiles. Cancelling an active Stripe subscription immediately, with no refund, is part of the same request. Deleting the Student, keeping identified attempt rows, or blocking deletion until the student cancels were rejected.
