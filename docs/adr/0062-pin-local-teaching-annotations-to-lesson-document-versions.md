# Pin local teaching annotations to lesson document versions

On shared teaching iPads, a Tutor's editable annotations belong to that Tutor, Session and downloaded document version; worksheet and solutions retain independent markings and viewing positions. V1 keeps this material on the iPad across sign-out, available again to the same Tutor after sign-in, and shares worksheet or solutions through separate explicit exports. A later replacement of the source PDF does not replace an annotated lesson's document or remap its ink: the Tutor may explicitly start a fresh copy. This preserves annotation alignment and separates local teaching material from confirmed Tutor logs and any future Student delivery; cached teaching can continue offline while new resource selection and log submission require a connection.
