# Version full-mock composition blueprints

Full UCAT mocks reference a versioned, immutable, test-year-specific UCAT exam blueprint that owns section totals, separate instruction and answering timings, and category or structure composition ranges. The blueprint is separate from taxonomy and from individual sets: focused practice sets may use any composition, while a full mock cannot publish unless its selected section sets satisfy its blueprint; this preserves historical mock rules when future UCAT formats change. Existing mocks are not blanket-attached to a new blueprint: a read-only eligibility audit records every gate result, unresolved Decision Making category classification remains provisional, and a tutor confirms attachment without silently recomposing shared sets.
