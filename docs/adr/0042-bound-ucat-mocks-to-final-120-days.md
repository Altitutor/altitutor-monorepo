# Bound UCAT mocks to the final 120 days

The standard study-plan mock schedule begins 120 days before the exam and contains at most 20 targets: four from days 120–61, four from days 60–29, and twelve in the final 28 days. No mock is scheduled in the final two recovery days.

These are fixed target offsets rather than a rounded per-window frequency. Readiness, prior completion, recovery spacing, and limited availability may reduce a student's total; they never increase it beyond 20. Outside the final 28 days, a rolling plan also retains at least one eligible study day for learning or section work instead of allowing mocks to occupy every available day.

This makes the catalogue requirement explicit: 20 unique published mocks cover the maximum standard trajectory without an asset repeat. The existing ten therefore require ten additional mocks, not a catalogue whose size grows with preparation horizons longer than 120 days.
