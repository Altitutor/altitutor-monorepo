# Altitutor 2026 UCAT exam blueprint

This is Altitutor's accepted composition policy for full 2026 UCAT mocks. Section totals and timings are exact. Category and presentation targets are Altitutor-authored ranges or preferences unless an official source explicitly establishes them; they are not universal restrictions on focused practice sets.

## Composition units

- A **question** is one candidate-visible response unit. A Decision Making five-statement drag-and-drop item and a Situational Judgement Most/Least screen each count as one question regardless of raw marks or placements.
- A **stem** is one shared passage, scenario, or information presentation with one or more questions.
- Full mocks must satisfy exact section totals. Builders optimise toward preferred values while staying within allowed ranges.

## Verbal Reasoning

- Exact total: **44 questions**
- Exact time: **22 minutes**
- Separate instruction time: **1 minute 30 seconds**
- Exact structure: **11 stems**

| Category | Allowed stems |
| --- | ---: |
| Reading Comprehension | 7–9 |
| True, False, Can't Tell | 2–4 |

## Decision Making

- Exact total: **35 questions**
- Exact time: **37 minutes**
- Separate instruction time: **1 minute 30 seconds**

| Canonical category | Teaching label | Preferred questions | Allowed questions |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: |
| Syllogisms | Simple syllogisms | — | 5–7 |
| Logical Puzzles | Logic Games | — | 5–6 |
| Recognising Assumptions | Strongest Argument | 4 | 3–5 |
| Interpreting Information and Drawing Conclusions | Longer syllogisms | — | 5–6 |
| Venn Diagrams | Venn Diagrams | 8 | 7–9 |
| Probabilistic and Statistical Reasoning | Probability | 5 | 4–6 |

Within Interpreting Information and Drawing Conclusions:

- Passage: **3–4 questions**
- Table or Graph/chart: **1–2 questions**
- Remaining questions, if any, may use Diagram/image, Mixed, or Other.

## Quantitative Reasoning

- Exact total: **36 questions**
- Exact time: **26 minutes**
- Separate instruction time: **2 minutes**
- Multi-question stems: **7–8**
- Single-question stems: **4–8**

The exact question total takes precedence; the builder selects whole stems while satisfying both structural ranges.

## Situational Judgement

- Exact total: **69 questions**
- Exact time: **26 minutes**
- Separate instruction time: **1 minute 30 seconds**
- Most/Least Appropriate preferred: **3 questions**
- Most/Least Appropriate allowed: **2–4 questions**
- Rating questions: the remainder needed to reach 69, with **66 preferred** and **65–67 allowed**
- Maximum questions per scenario stem: **6**

Every Most/Least Appropriate stem contains one question with exactly three actions and two distinct required placements: one Most Appropriate and one Least Appropriate.

## Enforcement

- Set creation displays blueprint targets and may auto-select whole stems to satisfy them.
- Ordinary and focused practice sets may publish outside the blueprint.
- A full mock tied to this blueprint cannot publish unless each section set satisfies the exact totals and allowed ranges.
- Later edits immediately recalculate and display blueprint compliance for every linked mock.

## Existing mocks

- Existing mocks and sets start without a blueprint reference; the migration does not silently claim that legacy content meets the 2026 policy.
- A deterministic eligibility audit checks section order, exact totals, separate timings, category ranges, presentation-format ranges, stem cardinalities, publication state, and response-contract validity.
- Unresolved Decision Making category classification or presentation format produces a provisional review result rather than an automatic attachment.
- Because sets may be shared by several mocks, the audit never retimes or recomposes a set automatically.
- A tutor confirms attachment after all hard gates pass. Failed and provisional checks remain visible with stable reason codes.
