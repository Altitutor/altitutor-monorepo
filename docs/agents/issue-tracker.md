## All manually started and scheduled agent work

Resolve or create the canonical issue before implementation. Explicit requests
can authorize new work; routine scope clarification belongs in that same issue.
Record the user's requested outcome, scope and decisions, then a claim containing
owner, UTC timestamp, branch/worktree, candidate SHA and next action. Check active
claims and preserve other agents' work. Reread immediately before edits and keep
human changes and prior evidence.

Publish material progress as dated Linear comments and keep the description's
current disposition accurate. Before finishing or pausing, persist the completed
work, exact commands/results and limits, candidate commit/PR, remaining step and
owner, and resume condition. Attach useful sanitized screenshots and evidence;
local paths or collapsed chat history alone are not a portable handoff.

A human review request needs the concrete question, expected behavior, evidence,
a pinned commit/version and a usable review/preview link. Record preview URL,
affected app, exact SHA/version and access/expiry limits. A localhost URL is not a
portable iPad preview. Do not claim an agent-owned process survives the task.
Phase two will provide previews independent of agent lifetime; until then, report
that limitation and retain a reviewable commit/PR/version in Linear.
